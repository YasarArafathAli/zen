About us

What we do

Our Business Divisions

Three focused areas serving industrial operations and procurement requirements.

01 / INDUSTRIAL SYSTEMS

Automatic Centralized Lubrication & Oil Filtration Systems

Industrial lubrication solutions designed to deliver lubricant to machine points and support equipment maintenance, along with oil filtration systems to help maintain oil cleanliness.

  • Lubricant routed to the points a machine needs
  • Filtration to help keep lubricating oil clean
  • Discussed against the plant, the duty, and the maintenance routine
02 / ENERGY EFFICIENCY

Fuel Additives for Boilers & Furnaces

Fuel-additive solutions intended to support boiler and furnace efficiency and help optimise consumption of coal, diesel and biomass. Results depend on fuel, equipment and operating conditions.

  • Aimed at boilers and furnaces
  • Discussed for coal, diesel, and biomass firing
  • No fixed saving is promised before the duty is known
03 / INDUSTRIAL PROCUREMENT

Industrial Scrap Purchasing

Procurement of industrial scrap, including ferrous and non-ferrous material and plant and machinery. We welcome enquiries from factories and organisations disposing of surplus, obsolete or redundant industrial assets.

  • Ferrous and non-ferrous industrial scrap
  • Plant and machinery that is surplus or obsolete
  • Enquiries from factories and asset owners
